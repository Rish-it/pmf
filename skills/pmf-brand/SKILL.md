# pmf-brand

A brand is the promise a customer remembers. This makes it specific to the
positioning the pack already found, not a mood board of nice colours.

## Step 1: read the brief

`pmf/marketing.md` (positioning, first audience), `pmf/research/`
(competitor names and looks to stay clear of), `pmf/panel/results.md` (what
buyers say they value). If positioning is missing, say so and offer
`pmf-marketing` first.

## Step 2: name candidates

Five to eight candidates, each scored on the same lines:

| check | the standard |
| --- | --- |
| Say and spell | survives being said once on a call, spelled without asking |
| Remember | two syllables beat four; a concrete word beats an abstract one |
| Fit | reads like the positioning, not like a random startup |
| Room | nothing in `pmf/research/competitors.csv` sounds confusingly close |

Then the checks to run before falling in love with the winner, each actually
performed when a research tool is available and otherwise listed as
user homework: domain availability, handles on the platforms that matter for
this buyer, the name in the relevant trademark register (search the register;
say that a lawyer confirms), and a plain web search for collisions.

## Step 3: voice

Three rules for how the brand writes and three for how it never writes, each
with a real example line using the buyers' words from
`pmf/research/voice.md`. Voice follows from the buyers, not from adjectives:
"warm, confident" is a mood; "short sentences, no exclamation marks, prices
always shown" is a voice.

## Step 4: the promise and the taglines

The promise: one line a customer could repeat (what they get, for whom, by
when). Then three tagline options: one plain, one playful, one that answers
the top objection. No invented claims, no superlatives.

## Step 5: the look brief

Four to six bullets a designer or a template can execute without asking
questions: colours with a reason and their use, type feel, imagery to avoid
(the stock imagery of this market from `competitors.csv`), and the first three
places the brand appears (from the marketing plan). This is a brief, not a
logo.

## Output

`pmf/brand.md`: scored name candidates and the checks run, voice rules with
examples, the promise and taglines, the look brief.
